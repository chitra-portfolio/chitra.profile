Product Readiness

Product Enablement Bill of Materials (BOM)

Challenge: Set the product-readiness standard for 4 post-sales functions.

Solution: Audited the Highspot library (16K+ views across 189 reps) and interviewed every role to map what they do and what they need to know. Built two frameworks from the findings: the Product Enablement Bill of Materials, a launch-stage map of the product knowledge each role needs, and an L1/L2/L3 learning framework covering universal, role-based, and technical deep-dive content.

Impact: Found 70% of audited content stale. Presented the framework to the Chief Customer Officer at the end of the first month and earned strong endorsement. Product and Product Marketing adopted it into GTM readiness materials, and CX, PMM, and Sales now reference it as core content.
